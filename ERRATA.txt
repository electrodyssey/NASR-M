
Issue: No console output on RS-232 port
Workaround: The TRS3221ECPW RS-232 transceiver won't transmit until FORCEOFF and FORCEON pins are HIGH. 
Short pins 15(VCC) and 16(*FORCEOFF), place resistors R145, R146.

Issue: I2C EEPROM U8 SCA & SCL lines are swapped.
Workaround: either rework or unsolder it

Issue: L4 shall be replaced with an inductor of a greater saturation current, at least 5A.
Workaround: Replacements - FTC303015D1R5MBCA(C53281631), SMTC303020-1R5MT (C22463870), SPM3020-1R5MA (C7471176), FTC303020D1R5MBCA (C7423317), FTC303018D1R5MBCA (C18724425), YSMC0320H-1R5MB (C41405950). Replace the R51 resistor with a 22K to enable 6.44A limit.

Issue: The secondary power supply won't start in automatic PWM/PSM mode.
Workaround: Remove R44 and place R45 to force the PWM mode.

Issue: I forgot to put a transistor switch to light up the LED's on +1V35 and +1V0_SYS power buses.
Workaround: Remove D15, R17, D13, R64. +3V3_SYS led is enough because it will be lit last.

Issue: The master TCXO (Y3 - ECS-TXO-32CSMV-270-AN-TR) outputs a 0.8V sine which is not enough to drive the PLL chip.
Workaround: Temporary workaround is rework. The replacements are ASTX-H11-25.000MHZ-T and FT3MHUPM25.0-T1. Future board revision will include one of them.
Workaround++: Replace R127 with a 1uF capacitor and watch the clipped sine appear on the output pin. AC termination magic!

Issue: R184 connected to PS_MIO1_500 of the SOC is 330Ohm. It looks like a copy-paste error.
Workaround: replace with a 10k resistor

Issue: SW5 PS Reset button shall be connected to PS_SRST_EXT_1.8 instead of PS_RST_B
Workaround: should be fixed in a future release

Issue: PHY_LED0 must be tied down to ground to enable the internal 1V0 in PS ETH PHY.
Workaround: Remove DNP flag from R272, mark R271 as DNP. Unsolder R271, solder in R272.

Issue: PHY MDI tracks shall be on an outter layer to achieve a better SI
Workaround: I'll be fixing that in a new board re-spin
